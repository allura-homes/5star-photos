Designer & printer handoffProof before production.

From screen to stock.

Neon screen color, glow and glass do not reproduce exactly in ink. Treat the RGB values as the visual target, then proof the conversion with your printer.

Finished size3.5 × 2 in / 88.9 × 50.8 mm (US). Confirm the printer’s template.
BleedTypically 0.125 in / 3.175 mm per edge. Document: 3.75 × 2.25 in. Extend the background fully.
Safe areaMinimum 0.125 in inside trim; 0.1875–0.25 in preferred. Concepts use 0.2 in.
Resolution300 ppi at final size for images; retain vector text. Do not print from a screenshot.
Color & PDFPrinter-supplied ICC profile. PDF/X-4 if accepted, with fonts embedded and the requested output intent.
Stock & finishSuggested: 16–18 pt matte or soft-touch. Optional spot UV on the badge; proof on actual stock.
