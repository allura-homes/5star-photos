# User Guide

Welcome to **5star.photos** — AI photo enhancement for short-term-rental hosts. This guide mirrors the in-app help at `/help`.

## The three-step workflow

Your best listing photo is about a minute away.

### 1. Upload
Go to **Library** and add a photo:
- **Drag & drop** (or click to browse) a JPG, PNG, WebP, or HEIC up to 50 MB.
- **Import from Airbnb** with the Airbnb button to pull existing listing photos.
- Or, on an empty library, click **Try a sample photo** to test the flow instantly.

The app automatically detects whether a photo is indoor or outdoor and enhances accordingly.

### 2. Enhance
Open a photo and press **Transform**. The app runs several AI models at the same time and shows each result as a **variation** next to your original. An AI "Art Director" writes a custom enhancement plan for each photo — brightening, color correction, and cleanup — while preserving what's actually in the shot (it won't invent pools, lawns, or furniture).

Compare the variations, then **save** the ones you like. If a particular model is temporarily unavailable, the others still run and you'll see a short note rather than a crash.

### 3. Download
Open a saved variation and **download the high-resolution** version, ready to upload to your listing.

## Enhancing many photos at once
Use **Batch Enhance** to process a whole set of photos in one go. Each photo runs through the models and lands in your library when it's done.

## Projects
Group related photos (for example, all shots of one property) into a **Project** from your library so they stay organized.

## Your account
- **Account** shows how many photos and saved variations you have, your plan and credit balance (with upgrade, top-up and manage-billing buttons), and your recent activity. You can set a display name and sign out here.
- **Activity** shows your full history of uploads, transforms, and downloads.

## Credits & pricing
Everything runs on credits. A new account starts with **45 free credits** — enough for three finished photos, no card needed.

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Upload a photo | 1 |
| Transform (every model in your plan, in one go) | 10 |
| Save a variation as a working image | 1 |
| Download hi-res | 3 |

If a transform produces no result at all, the 10 credits are refunded automatically.

**Plans** refill your credits every month and can be billed monthly or annually (two months free): Start-up ($19, 100 credits, V1 + V2 models), Pro ($49, 270 credits, every model) and Max ($99, 570 credits, every model). Subscribers can also buy **top-up packs** (50 / 150 / 300 credits) that never expire while you are subscribed. Unused monthly credits do not roll over. Change or cancel your plan anytime from **Account → Manage billing**; upgrades apply immediately, downgrades and cancellations at the end of the period. See `/pricing` in the app for current prices.

## Tips for the best results
- Start from the sharpest, most level photo you have; AI enhancement improves lighting and color but can't recover a very blurry shot.
- Shoot in landscape for listing hero images.
- Try more than one variation — different models flatter different rooms.
- For exteriors, an overcast original often enhances into a bright, natural-looking result.

## Trouble?
- **A model didn't produce a result.** Some models occasionally hit provider limits. Try again, or use one of the other variations.
- **Upload failed.** Very large files are compressed automatically; if a photo still won't upload, try re-exporting it as a JPG.
- **Login is stuck.** Refresh the page and sign in again. If it persists, sign out fully and back in.
